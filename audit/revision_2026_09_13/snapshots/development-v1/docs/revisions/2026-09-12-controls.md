# Whitening, position controls, and expansion scheduling

The primary model/temperature/layer/statistic cell remains unchanged. The new diagnostic
grid is secondary and reports negative or inconclusive results without selecting a winner.
It crosses shrinkage 0.05/0.1/0.2, same versus disjoint transform/threshold calibration tasks,
and keeping versus removing the first increment. Splits depend on a fixed task-ID hash.
Removing the first increment removes it from transform fitting, threshold fitting and
evaluation; original step indices remain intact.

For every score, jump and surprisal use exactly the same finite traces. Report full traces,
failures after the first step, and those same failures with the first candidate removed.
Excluded first failures, missing targets and nonfinite pairs are counted. Task bootstrap
moves all attempts together and is unavailable below twenty tasks. These are descriptive,
conditional sensitivity intervals; they do not establish a new confirmatory family.
The family/length positional null preserves actual step coordinates after exclusions.
Sparse strata remain excluded and cannot be pooled to manufacture significance.

Historical pilot controls retain their old measurement lineage. New pilot and main controls
wait for the repaired Lean campaign. All new outputs have separate directories and manifests.
The previous main refits are redirected to the repaired measurements. The original primary
analysis and all original generations remain unchanged.

The user authorized pilot deduction before paired P4. Only two never-started pending P4
requests are cancelled and resubmitted later; running original generations are preserved.
Pilot GPU jobs use a two-hour request and nice=0; P4 keeps its original runtime/configuration
and uses nice=0 after pilot extraction. The expansion GPU submission cap remains two.
P4 consists of five fixed real/null seed pairs, followed by existing matched-frequency
remeasurement and tail analysis. Controller receipt and checks are under
audit/controls_2026_09_12. Queue admission is distinct from GPU allocation or completed science.
