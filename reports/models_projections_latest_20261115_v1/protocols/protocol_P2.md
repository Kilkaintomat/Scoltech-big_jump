## Fitting and evaluation
Use the same conditional-logit, scalar baseline, grids, scaling, nested direction fitting and group weights as Part1. Baseline includes position, relative position, first/last indicators, step-token length, surprisal spline and signed/absolute change. No tactic-type extra block is added: all rows use constant OTHER. This is the requested common baseline, not the richer historic geometry baseline; archival numbers are not presented as matched effects.
Fit only nD and nH in Part2; weak whole-proof supervision was a Part1-specific extra. Logistic risk negatives are accepted steps before first failure plus verified-proof steps; never post-failure. Coordinate normalization and all hyperparameter choices use only the relevant training subset. Grid[.0001,.001,.01,.1,1,10], ties choose larger lambda; nested5x3 with further3fold direction selection exactly as Part1.
Same statistics,20fixed random directions, conditional localizers, prefix C2, max-statistic AUROC and whole-proof baseline+statistic detector as Part1. No PRM scores invented for Lean. For transferred directions, direction and orientation are fixed from source; downstream scalar parameters use target OOF fitting, clearly labelled. Additionally report entirely frozen ProofNet predictors on miniF2F and Putnam as exploratory tests, without target labels for their coefficients.
Full source directions are tuned/refit on all eligible source evaluation training groups, excluding exact target-task overlap. Native OOF uses separate training-only normals at every outer and inner fold. Cross-domain PB full directions use Part1 evaluation2361 corpus only, not the calibration labels. Native Lean directions pool author corpora.
Direction/cosine comparisons do not prove a single sufficient or causal axis. Fold training subsets overlap. Operational norm dilution compares raw norm with its raw-coordinate perpendicular component, and requires no two-sided significant perpendicular addition in either endpoint.
No positive/negative labels or sufficient folds -> explicit unavailable cell, p=1 in frozen family. No AUC with a single outcome; no CI below20 independent groups (AUC also20 contributing groups per class). Never invent correct Putnam proofs.
