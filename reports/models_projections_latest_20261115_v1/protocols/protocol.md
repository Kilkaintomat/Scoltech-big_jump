## Frozen splits and estimators
Retain archived calibration/evaluation assignment. Outer evaluation failed-task fold IDs are copied from E16/fits.json (same evaluation traces); evaluation groups absent from that archive (correct-only or exclusively L=1) assigned int(SHA256("2026111517/"+group),16)%5. Hence any task, across all its outcomes, is excluded from its test direction. Inner and direction-tuning subfolds use GroupKFold(3) on sorted unique task IDs, not step rows. This algorithm and generated split manifest are frozen before fits. For new datasets Part 2 use GroupKFold(5) on unique task groups, inner3.
Equal task weight, equal attempt within task, equal step within at-risk attempt for hazard fitting. Risk set includes all steps of correct solutions and steps through first error of failed ones; no post-error negatives. Direction models: intercept + coordinate-standardized raw d or raw X; standardized L2 logistic regression. Positive = first error. Weak label direction: standardized mean X across entire solution, one binary outcome per solution; no step labels; its state-plane score and delta score are both reported (state score is primary weak-label statistic).

## Statistical families and rules
Fixed predictions, 10000 whole-task bootstrap draws; equal-task averages (equal attempts within task); percentile95% intervals. No interval <20 contributing task groups; AUROC additionally >=20 groups in each class. Bootstrap seed2026111517; cached task draw indices shared across paired contrasts. Two-sided centered-bootstrap p=(1+count(abs(draw-point)>=abs(point)))/(B+1), Holm.
Part 1 primary family pools all 6 contrasts x 2 endpoints(Hit,NLL) x 5 populations(pooled and four named subdatasets) x 9 model/readout contexts(7 PB-trained and 2 frozen-Lean primary-layer contexts): 540 hypotheses, including unavailable hypotheses with p=1. Six contrasts: baseline+sD minus baseline+w, baseline+abs(sD) minus baseline+w, baseline+sH minus baseline+w, baseline+h minus baseline, baseline+perpendicular-norm minus baseline, median over20 baseline+random-direction metrics minus baseline. This joint family prevents selecting Hit vs NLL or a favorable dataset after inspection. Median random uses median METRIC, not selecting a direction. Generator-family strata, secondary layers, PRM, weak labels, AUROC, prefix and other contrasts are exploratory in a separate Holm family; secondary unavailable outputs retained. Individual CIs are not Holm-adjusted simultaneous CIs; report both CIs and adjusted p.
Decision rules exactly as attachment: directed signal requires positive signed-projection-vs-norm standalone CI AND replacing norm significant after Holm(Hit OR NLL); sign matters iff signed-minus-absolute standaloneCI>0; operational norm-dilution rule requires more norm/perp argmax agreement than norm/signed and no significant perpendicular addition (not equivalence evidence); transfer requires significant adjusted baseline gain; Lean->informal requires positive Lean-projection-vs-norm and vspositionCI plus point above medianrandom; weaklabel rule requires positivevsnorm/positionCIs. Report every negative/inconclusive.
Fold cosine matrices for H,D,weak and Lean/PB, reference20 random pairs; not causal alignment.
